Machine analysis of the first 35 seconds found tonal audio from the opening. Around 0:16 the signal became less periodic and had more energy above 3 kHz. The offline Hebrew speech model did not recover a reliable lyric in the excerpt. A listening pass is still needed to identify any audible crowd, the end of the intro, and the first sung line.

For each selection, retain written permission identifying the exact master, the composition or arrangement, permission to make and distribute the backing, and offline bundling terms. If permission is unavailable, use a separately recorded lead and backing on a documented cleared, familiar melody. Record the take and arrangement credits alongside the new recording.

Before featuring a tune, have a Hebrew reviewer check all sung lines, pronunciation, English, and repeats. Test each tune with five listeners who have not seen the interface. Record how many recognize it and reach the first sung line unaided. The acceptance threshold is four of five per tune. No recognition trial or human review has been recorded here.
